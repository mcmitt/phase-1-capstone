Classic American Flavors
Memphis
Wings

Spiced to order with cayenne oil and flavored with garlic, these are a clasice favorite!

Carolina
Wings

Rolled in a mustard-based carolina gold sauce, over 80% of orders include these!

Kansas City
Wings

With a sweet heat that grows every so slowly, we run out of these at least once a week!

Texas
Wings

Dry rubbed with black pepper, garlic, and paprika these wings are servced with texas sauce on the side and are amazingly dippable!

Cajun
Wings

Rubbed with cajun blackening spices and tosed with sweet fermented cayenne peppers. Not exactly hot, but a deep heat!

Lemongrass

Mild, sweet, and tangy. Lemongrass, white pepper, and tossed with scallions. These wings are winning!

Banana
Wings

Yes, you read that right. Spiced with a touch of curry and tossed with fresh jalapeños, these will pleasantly surprise!

Grilled
Wings

Looking for the lighter side? As a lower calorie option these grilled wings won't disappoint!

Wing
Soup

Delicious wing meat in a delectable bisque broth. Weird, but your stomach will thank you!

Kids
Wings

Deep fried boneless wings for the kiddos. Any wings sauce on the side, or ketchup (most common) So good they won't ask for dessert!

European Favorites
Parmesan
Wings

Mild cream sauce of cream, garlic, a touch of red pepper, and of course parmesan. A classic favorite the we can't make enough of!

Tuscan
Wings

Thick and creamy touting the flavors of oregano, thyme, basil, roesmary and the depth of flavor from sun dried tomatoes. Mild and mouth watering!

Pepperonata
Wings

Imagine a sauce of stewed peppers, tomatoes, onions, and garlic with just a few chilis for slow building heat. This sauce embodies summer in Italy!

Turkish
Wings

Based on Iskender, this sauce is a tomato-based with oregano, red and black pepper and a touch of yoghurt. Requested by one of our original patrons from Turkey, this recipe is loved by all true foodies!

Far East Favorites
Korean
Wings

Sweet heat with a flair of sesame and red pepper, tossed with scallions. These are a favorite!

Mongolian
Wings

Deep, mellow richness and sweet with a slow building heat. Tossed with scallions - a definite crowd pleaser!

Orange
Wings

Thick and mild with the flavor of orange zest and dashed with sesame seeds. Unbelievably savory and rich!

Thai
Wings

Drowned in a curry-based sauce with thai chilis to bring the heat. Tossed with scallions. These can cure all ailments!**
