About Me:

I'm currently between jobs being previously employed by my father, Niccolo, and my uncle Maffeo. Our family-owned business was a startup with the vision of modernizing trade between Europe and the far east over the well known silk road.

Projects:

Our projects were very interesting, here is a brief timeline and summary of the projects we undertook:

  • Trade Negotiations: 1271-1275
    Traveling overland from Venice, Italy in Europe across the Silk Road to Asia, we arrived in China and met our business partner Kublai Khan. He was the grandson of Genghis Khan, who managed trade for the Mongol Empire.
  • Trade Modernization: 1275-1292
    With the help and permission of Mr. Khan, I worked as a temp performing the work of an envoy to the various cities, towns, villages, hamlets, and neighborhoods throughout the empire. I gained quite a bit of knowledge and experience and was able to compile a strategy to modernize trade based on the region's geography, customs, and culture.
  • Selling the Book Rights: 1295-1296
    After returning to Venice, our company had become influential, and the CEO, my father, and the COO, my uncle, had become quite wealthy. I also had gathered a modest living from our projects, but I ran into trouble with the law and spent some time in jail. But while I was in prison I sold the book rights to my story so that when I was released I had a good retirement planned.
